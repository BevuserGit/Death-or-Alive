Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2025

Death or Alive ("we," "our," or "us") is a browser-based prank game designed purely for entertainment. We are committed to respecting your privacy and protecting any information you may provide during your brief interaction with the game.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it’s used, and your rights.

Introduction

This game is not a commercial service or platform. It is an interactive, client-side experience designed for fun and parody purposes only. It does not require registration, login, or account creation.

What Information Do We Collect?

a. Temporarily Entered Info
During gameplay, we may ask for:

  • First Name (for story immersion)
  • Age (to adjust content appropriately)
  • Gender (to personalize certain video content for users age 14 and above)

    • This data is:
  • Not stored or saved beyond your current session.
  • Not sent to a server or shared with any third party.
  • Used only to customize your experience locally in your browser.

    • b. Log Data and Cookies

    We do not actively collect or store log data. However, third-party services (like Google AdSense) may collect basic usage data such as:

  • IP address
  • Device/browser type
  • Pages visited
  • Duration of visit

    • Cookies may be used by these services to personalize ads.

    Children's Privacy

    While the game includes a safeguard to avoid showing mature content to users under 14, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years old. If you are a parent and believe your child has entered personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

    Third-Party Services

    We may use third-party services (like AdSense or embedded content providers) that may collect non-personal data. These third parties operate independently and are governed by their own privacy policies.

    Security

    This game does not store personal information or use backend servers. As no data is collected or transmitted by us, there is no stored information at risk. However, third-party services may have their own data policies.

    Updates to This Policy

    This policy may be updated periodically. The most recent version will always be available on this page.

    Contact Us

    If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us:

    • By email: support@deathoralive.com
    • By visiting the contact page on our website: Contact Us
